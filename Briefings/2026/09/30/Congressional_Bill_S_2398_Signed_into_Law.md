# Congressional Bill S. 2398 Signed into Law

On Wednesday, September 30, 2026, the President signed into law:

S. 2398, the “Kay Hagan Tick Reauthorization Act,” which reauthorizes through Fiscal Year 2030, activities to address vector-borne diseases.
