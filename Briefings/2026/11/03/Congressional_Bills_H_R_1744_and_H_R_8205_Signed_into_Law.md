# Congressional Bills H.R. 1744 and H.R. 8205 Signed into Law

On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the President signed into law:

H.R. 1744, the “United States Commission on International Religious Freedom Reauthorization Act of 2026,” which extends and authorizes annual appropriations for the United States Commission on International Religious Freedom through Fiscal Year 2028; and

H.R. 8205, the “Accelerating Access to Critical Therapies for ALS Reauthorization Act of 2026,” which reauthorizes the Accelerating Access to Critical Therapies for ALS Act through Fiscal Year 2031.
