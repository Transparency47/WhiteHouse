# First Lady Melania Trump Honors America’s Fallen Heroes at the Tomb of The Unknown Soldier

The First Lady of the United States of America, Mrs. Melania Trump, participated in a wreath-laying ceremony at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier at Arlington National Cemetery on Veterans Day.

The First Lady reflected on the experience:

“Every time I visit the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, I use the surrounding calmness to reflect on our nation’s military. Yesterday’s visit was no different.

I think about the Americans who gave their lives defending our freedom. I think about their families, their loved ones, and the lives America’s Unknown Soldiers never had a chance to experience. We lost fathers, mothers, innovators, leaders of justice, athletes, musicians, and so much more.

The Tomb of the Unknown Soldier is also important because it represents the diversity of our nation. Brave Americans from every background, race, and religion served in the name of freedom. Together, these unknown souls are forever united by their ultimate commitment to our country.

The soldiers who guard the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, every hour of every day, year after year, are highly dedicated individuals. They refuse to abandon their fallen brothers and sisters. Let these individuals inspire our children about the core value of American commitment.

I will always remember the sacrifice of our Unknown Soldiers. They gave their lives for all of us, and for future generations of Americans.”
