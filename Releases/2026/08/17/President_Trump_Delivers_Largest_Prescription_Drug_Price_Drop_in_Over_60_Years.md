# President Trump Delivers Largest Prescription Drug Price Drop in Over 60 Years

**President Donald J. Trump promised to slash prescription drug prices — and he has delivered.** [New data](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm) confirms prescription drug prices just recorded their largest annual decline in more than 60 years, the steepest drop since 1963, as the Trump Administration’s landmark [Most Favored Nation](https://www.whitehouse.gov/research/2026/05/savings-from-most-favored-nation-drug-pricing-policy/) policies take hold.

**President Trump is the** [**first President**](https://x.com/RapidResponse47/status/2087949207285981226) **to actually lower prescription drug prices — a direct result of his bold action.** Through [Most Favored Nation](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/05/delivering-most-favored-nation-prescription-drug-pricing-to-american-patients/) pricing, 17 of the world’s largest drugmakers — covering approximately 86% of the branded drug market — have agreed to bring U.S. prices in line with the lowest paid by comparable developed nations, ending decades of Americans subsidizing cheaper drugs abroad and forcing fair pricing for patients at home.

**The Trump Administration’s revolutionary** [**TrumpRx**](https://trumprx.gov/) **platform has already generated** [**$700 million**](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/americans-save-more-than-700m-prescription-medications-through-trumprx-white-house-says) **in savings for millions of American patients.** GLP-1 medications that once cost over $1,000 a month are now available starting as low as $149. Fertility drugs, inhalers, insulin, cholesterol treatments, and dozens of other high-cost drugs have seen cuts of 50–90% or more.

**The results are clear:**

- Prescription drug prices are down 3.9% since President Trump took office and have fallen [every month](https://www.moneytalksnews.com/drug-prices-just-had-their-biggest-drop-since-but-your-medical-bills-keep-climbing/#:~:text=Let%E2%80%99s%20start%20with%20the%20good%20news%2C%20because%20there%E2%80%99s%20plenty%20of%20it.%20According%20to%20the%20Bureau%20of%20Labor%20Statistics%2C%20prescription%20drug%20prices%20fell%200.8%25%20in%20July%20alone%20%E2%80%94%20and%20by%20the%20government%E2%80%99s%20own%20tally%2C%20they%20haven%E2%80%99t%20risen%20in%20a%20single%20month%20this%20year.) of 2026. Nonprescription drugs are down 2.4% over the same period.

- Medical care commodities, including equipment and supplies, are down 2.7% over the past year.

- The Department of Veterans Affairs has locked in more than [$10 billion](https://news.va.gov/press-room/va-secures-10b-in-pharmaceutical-price-reductions-so-far-in-fy-2026/) in pharmaceutical savings this fiscal year alone.

- Hundreds of billions more in long-term savings are [projected](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Savings-from-MFN-Drug-Pricing-Policy.pdf) from the full Most Favored Nation framework.
