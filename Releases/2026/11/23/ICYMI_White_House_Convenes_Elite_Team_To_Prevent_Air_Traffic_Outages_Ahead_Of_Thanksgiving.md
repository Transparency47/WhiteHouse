# ICYMI: “White House Convenes Elite Team To Prevent Air Traffic Outages Ahead Of Thanksgiving”

**As millions of Americans prepare to travel for the Thanksgiving holiday, President Donald J. Trump is making sure the skies stay open and the systems run smoothly.** Under President Trump’s leadership, the White House has assembled an elite, interagency team to keep America’s air traffic control systems strong and reliable throughout the holiday season.

**From** [**The Daily Wire**](https://www.dailywire.com/news/white-house-convenes-elite-team-to-prevent-air-traffic-outages-ahead-of-thanksgiving) **:**

*“As Americans prepare for Thanksgiving travel, the White House has assembled a special team to prevent any further air traffic control outages, officials shared with The Daily Wire.*

*The White House assembled the ‘Transportation Technology Transformation Tiger Team’ in the wake of a malfunction that resulted in a grounding of all flights nationwide. The team — comprised of officials from the FAA, Departments of Transportation and Defense, and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence — has met every day this week to ensure continuity of service, a White House official shared…*

*White House spokeswoman Allison Schuster tells The Daily Wire Americans preparing to hit the road this week have nothing to worry about.*

*‘President Trump and [Transportation] Secretary [Sean] Duffy have brought together the best and the brightest across the government to ensure what happened last Friday can never happen again,’ Schuster said. ‘Work continues, and Americans can rest assured their country’s aviation is the best in the world.’”*
