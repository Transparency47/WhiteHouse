# Voting Begins for the 2026 National Thanksgiving Turkey Pardon Ceremony

President Donald J. Trump will celebrate a uniquely American Thanksgiving tradition as the White House hosts the 79th National Thanksgiving Turkey presentation and pardoning ceremony on Tuesday, November 24, 2026.

This year’s turkeys, named **Freedom** and **Feathers** by the American people via an online poll last week, were raised in West Virginia, under the direction of National Turkey Federation Chairwoman Christy Puffenbarger, by owners of Deans Gap Farm Scott and Becky Rightsell.

Each year, two turkeys are officially pardoned by the President of the United States, but only one receives the ceremonial honor at the White House. Following a final public poll on [WhiteHouse.Gov/Thanksgiving](https://www.whitehouse.gov/thanksgiving/), one of these two turkeys will be ceremonially pardoned at the White House on Tuesday, November 24, 2026. Both turkeys will live out there days at Texas A&M following the ceremony.

**The American public is now invited to decide which turkey will take center stage during the pardon ceremony hosted by President Trump by voting [here](https://www.whitehouse.gov/thanksgiving/#vote).**

**Voting will conclude the night of Monday, November 23, 2026, at 11:59 PM.**
